R3.5 Y26

Fixes stuck notes after changing the root note or chord multiple times. Note Off messages now match the exact notes and MIDI channel used at Note On.

When breath stops, fades volume to zero over 100 ms without blocking controls, then sends Note Off for all active notes. The fade also handles changes below the volume deadband. A new breath during the fade closes the old notes before the new attack. Entering setup releases notes immediately.

Omits chord tones above MIDI note 127 and removes debug text from MIDI output.

Synchronizes pitch centering and bend range with the synthesizer, including channel changes. Improves menu navigation, center-button debounce, playback after setup, and joystick repeat timing. Entering setup preserves the startup breath calibration.

Includes R3.5_Y26.hex for Arduino Mini ATmega168. Validated with a PlatformIO AVR build and host regression tests, including all 8,320 root/chord combinations. Physical sensor and synthesizer testing remains necessary.
